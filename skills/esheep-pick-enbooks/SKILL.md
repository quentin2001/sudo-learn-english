# 📚 English Book Selection Skill (esheep-pick-enbooks)

## 🎯 Overview
Based on Second Language Acquisition (SLA) $i+1$ theory and the Dialysis Reading Method (透析法), this skill provides a rigorous, data-backed standard for picking, evaluating, and filtering English books to guarantee **high comprehension (≥95%), zero-dictionary reading flow, and sustainable habit formation**.

---

## 🚫 The 3 Non-Negotiable Screening Rules (绝对筛除项)

When evaluating any English reading material, **REJECT IMMEDIATELY** if it violates any of the following:

1. ❌ **No Adapted/Simplified Readers (坚决拒绝简写本/改写本)**:
   * *Why*: Graded readers (e.g., Bookworm / 书虫, Bedside Readers / 床头灯) strip away natural authentic collocations and nuance, leaving behind dry, unnatural, skeleton sentences.
   * *Rule*: Read **ONLY Unabridged Original Works (未删节原著)** written by native authors for native readers.
2. ❌ **No Pre-20th-Century Classical Literature for Beginners (初中级坚决不碰古典名著)**:
   * *Why*: 18th~19th century classics (Austen, Dickens, Bronte sisters, Hugo, Tolstoy translations) contain archaic vocabulary, obsolete idioms, and overly convoluted Victorian syntax that cannot be transferred into modern spoken/written English.
   * *Rule*: Prioritize **contemporary works (published after 1990/2000)**.
3. ❌ **No "Must-Read Classics" Guilt-Trip (兴趣第一，拒绝为面子硬啃)**:
   * *Rule*: *"The end justifies the means."* If a bestselling page-turner (thriller, mystery, sci-fi, personal growth, business biography) keeps you hooked until 2 AM, it is 100x more valuable than a "literary masterpiece" you abandon on page 10.

## 📊 Quantitative Difficulty Metrics (量化评估指标)

```
                       [ STTR 首万词不重复词数 ]
   1000               1500               2000               2800+
     |------------------|------------------|------------------|
     |   Phase 1 入门    |   Phase 2 进阶    |   Phase 3 高阶   |  (不宜入门)
     | (心流/极简经管)   | (畅销悬疑/青少年) | (商业传记/严肃)  |
     | Cheese (1396)    | Narnia (1400-1800)| Steve Jobs (2225)| Wuthering
     | Prince (1613)    | HP 1 (1856)      | Godfather (2213) | Heights (2929)
```

### 1. STTR (Standardized Type-Token Ratio / 首万词不重复词数)
Measures vocabulary density and lexical variety in the first 10,000 words:
* **< 1,500 (Easy / Flow Starter)**: Low vocabulary friction. Perfect for building initial confidence and reading momentum.
* **1,500 ~ 2,000 (Intermediate / Mainstream)**: Standard contemporary fiction and popular non-fiction.
* **2,000 ~ 2,500 (Upper-Intermediate / Rich)**: Deep biographies, multi-layered fiction, business treatises.
* **> 2,500 (Advanced / Dense Literary)**: High lexical barrier. Requires solid dialectical endurance.

### 2. Lexile Measure (蓝思值)
Measures sentence length and syntactic complexity:
* **500L ~ 750L**: Elementary / Accessible (*The Little Prince: 710L*, *Twilight: 670L~720L*)
* **750L ~ 950L**: Intermediate / Sweet Spot (*Who Moved My Cheese: 880L*, *Harry Potter 1-4: 880L~950L*, *Foundation: 830L*)
* **950L ~ 1150L**: Upper-Intermediate (*Steve Jobs: 1080L*, *The Great Gatsby: 1070L*, *Gone with the Wind: 1100L*)
* **1150L+**: Academic / Dense Classical (*A Short History of Nearly Everything: 1190L*, *Robinson Crusoe: 1320L*)

## ⚡ The 2-Page Gold Test (3分钟黄金实测法)

Before committing to reading any candidate book:

1. Open the book to **Pages 3~5** (skip the prologue/preface).
2. Read **2 full pages continuously** without touching a dictionary.
3. Apply the Decision Matrix:

| Test Result | Diagnostic | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0 ~ 2 unknown words** & 100% flow | **Optimal $i+1$** | **✅ APPROVED** – Read in pure flow mode! |
| **3 ~ 5 unknown words**, plot is clear | **Manageable $i+1$** | **⚡ APPROVED** – Read with Dialysis Rule (max 1 lookup per 2 pages). |
| **> 5 unknown words** OR lost in plot | **High Friction ($i+3$)** | **🛑 REJECT / SHELVE** – Switch to an easier book immediately. |

## 🗺️ 3-Phase Recommended Progression Roadmap

### 🟢 Phase 1: Flow & Confidence (心流建立与极简启航)
* *Characteristics*: Short (<40k words), low STTR (<1600), high dialogue/story ratio.
* *Top Picks*:
  1. **Who Moved My Cheese?** (STTR 1396 | 880L | 10k words) – Business allegory
  2. **The Little Prince** (STTR 1613 | 710L | 17k words) – Philosophical fable
  3. **The Old Man and the Sea** (STTR 1554 | 940L | 27k words) – Hemingway's direct prose
  4. **The Chronicles of Narnia 1~2** (STTR 1419~1490 | 790L~940L) – Fantasy adventure
  5. **Rich Dad Poor Dad** (STTR 1799 | 860L | 57k words) – Financial mindset

### 🔵 Phase 2: Page-Turners & Genre Fiction (情节日记与语感加速)
* *Characteristics*: Highly addictive plot, contemporary language, 50k~150k words.
* *Top Picks*:
  1. **Harry Potter 1~4** (STTR 1856~2172 | 880L~950L) – Modern British fantasy
  2. **The Psychology of Money** (STTR ~1800 | 900L | 60k words) – Modern behavioral finance
  3. **Atomic Habits** (STTR ~1850 | 920L | 70k words) – Modern behavioral science
  4. **The Shawshank Redemption** (STTR 2036 | Stephen King) – Gripping novella
  5. **Death on the Nile** (STTR 2228 | 660L | 79k words) – Agatha Christie mystery

### 🟣 Phase 3: Business Epic & Thought Leadership (商业纪实与工作语言化)
* *Characteristics*: Real-world corporate insight, rich vocabulary, strategic thinking.
* *Top Picks*:
  1. **Shoe Dog** (Phil Knight) – Nike founder memoir, storytelling masterpiece
  2. **Bad Blood** (John Carreyrou) – Silicon Valley VC & investigative thriller
  3. **Steve Jobs** (STTR 2225 | 1080L | Walter Isaacson) – Tech innovation epic
  4. **Zero to One** (Peter Thiel) – Startup and venture capital first principles
  5. **Arthur Hailey's Industry Novels** (*The Moneychangers*, *Hotel*, *Airport*)
